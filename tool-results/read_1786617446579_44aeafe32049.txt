    66→    66→    66→- random statistics
    67→    67→    67→- charts with no purpose
    68→    68→    68→- icons beside everything
    69→    69→    69→- excessive pills
    70→    70→    70→- "AI Insight" boxes everywhere
    71→    71→    71→- five equally important buttons
    72→    72→    72→- symmetrical layouts everywhere
    73→    73→    73→- fake-looking avatars
    74→    74→    74→- fake-looking logos
    75→    75→    75→- placeholder content

    81→    81→    81→For every element ask:
    82→    82→    82→

   119→   119→   119→# 4. INFORMATION HIERARCHY
   120→   120→   120→
   121→   121→   121→Every screen needs:
   122→   122→   122→
   123→   123→   123→### Orientation
   124→   124→   124→Where am I? What entity? What state? What time/context?

   127→   127→   127→The single most important thing.
   128→   128→   128→
   129→   129→   129→### Supporting evidence
   130→   130→   130→Metrics, charts, events, tactical information, model factors.
   131→   131→   131→
   132→   132→   132→### Exploration
   133→   133→   133→Secondary information for deeper investigation.
   134→   134→   134→
   135→   135→   135→### Actions
   136→   136→   136→Actions placed where they become useful, not dumped at the top.
   137→   137→   137→
   138→   138→   138→Never make every section visually equal.
