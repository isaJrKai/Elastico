     7→## Purpose
     8→
     9→Determine how ELASTICO should use bookmaker market information, football information,
    10→and model probabilities to produce **calibrated, transparent, decision-useful predictions**.
    11→
    12→The core question: **"Can ELASTICO produce probabilities that are demonstrably better
    13→calibrated and/or more informative than the available market signal?"**
    14→
    15→This phase distinguishes four probability concepts that are often conflated:
    16→
    17→| Concept | Definition | Source |
    18→|---------|-----------|--------|
    19→| Market Probability | Normalized bookmaker implied probabilities | Closing odds (Pinnacle/B365) |
    20→| ELASTICO Model Probability | XGBoost/other model output | Trained on football + market features |
    21→| Calibrated Final Probability | Post-calibration model output | Platt/isotonic/temperature scaling |
    22→| Market Edge | Model prob minus market prob | Computed difference |
    23→
    24→## 5B — Market Probability Construction Audit
    25→
    26→### Data Source
    27→
    28→Bookmaker odds are sourced from **football-data.co.uk** CSV files.
    29→Primary source: **Pinnacle** (PSH/PSD/PSA). Fallback: **Bet365** (B365H/B365D/B365A).
    30→These are **closing odds** — the last available before kickoff.
    31→
    32→### Odds Conversion Method
    33→
    34→```
    35→raw_implied_home = 1 / odds_home
    36→raw_implied_draw = 1 / odds_draw
    37→raw_implied_away = 1 / odds_away
    38→sum_implied = raw_implied_home + raw_implied_draw + raw_implied_away  # > 1.0 (overround)
    39→
    40→# NORMALIZATION (overround removal):
    41→implied_prob_home = (1/odds_home) / sum_implied * 100
    42→implied_prob_draw = (1/odds_draw) / sum_implied * 100
    43→implied_prob_away = (1/odds_away) / sum_implied * 100
    44→overround = sum_implied * 100  # e.g., 105.2 means 5.2% margin
    45→```
    46→
    47→**Key findings from data audit:**
    48→
    49→- Overround: mean=103.0%, std=0.7%, range=[101.8%, 107.8%]
    50→- Probability sum: mean=100.00 (should be ~100.0 if normalized)
    51→- Available: 7,534 rows, Missing: 2 rows
    52→- Implied Prob Home: mean=44.0%
    53→- Implied Prob Draw: mean=24.7%
    54→- Implied Prob Away: mean=31.4%
    55→
    56→**IMPORTANT**: The implied probabilities in the dataset are **normalized** (overround-removed).
    57→They sum to exactly 100. This means they represent the bookmaker's probability estimate
    58→AFTER removing their margin, not the raw market-implied probability.
    59→
    60→**Known issue**: The inference code in `src/ml/features.py` produces **raw** (non-normalized)
    61→implied probabilities, creating a train/inference mismatch. This must be fixed before deployment.

   326→## 5E — Market Edge Analysis
   327→
   328→Market Edge = Model Probability - Market Probability for the model's predicted class.
   329→A positive edge means the model is more confident than the market in its predicted outcome.
   330→
   331→**Critical caveat**: A 1% probability difference should NOT automatically be treated
   332→as a betting opportunity. Edge must exceed the bookmaker margin (typically 3-6%) and
   333→be validated across sufficient sample sizes.

   515→
   516→- XGB Combined beats Raw Market on Log Loss: **0/5** windows
   517→- XGB Combined beats Raw Market on ECE: **1/5** windows
   518→- XGB Combined beats Raw Market on Accuracy: **0/5** windows
   519→- XGB Market-Only beats Raw Market on Log Loss: **0/5** windows

   522→- Log Loss delta: +0.1410 (negative = XGB better)
   523→- ECE delta: +0.0700 (negative = XGB better)
   524→- Accuracy delta: -0.0352 (positive = XGB better)

   528→## Conclusions & Recommendations
   529→
   530→### 1. Model Selection
   531→
   532→Best average log loss across all windows: **Raw Market** (0.9592)
   533→- Raw Market: 0.9592 ** <-- BEST**
   534→- XGB Market: 1.0968
   535→- XGB Combined: 1.1003
   536→- XGB Football: 1.1134
   537→
   538→### 2. Calibration Recommendation
   539→
   540→Temperature scaling converged to T=1.000 in ALL windows, meaning the XGBoost Combined
   541→model is already at its optimal temperature on the calibration set. This is expected
   542→because XGBoost with softprob output is already near-optimally calibrated.
   543→
   544→Platt and isotonic calibration HURT performance significantly (LL increases by 0.4-4.1).
   545→This is likely because they overfit the calibration set (especially for W1 where
   546→calibration data and test data overlap).
   547→
   548→**Key insight**: The calibration problem is not about post-hoc adjustment.
   549→The problem is that the model itself is miscalibrated relative to market probabilities.
   550→The solution is not calibration — it is using the market probabilities directly,
   551→or building a model that genuinely improves on them.
   552→
   553→### 3. Market Intelligence
   554→
   555→**Raw bookmaker probabilities dominate ALL models on ALL metrics in ALL windows.**
   556→They win on log loss 5/5, on ECE 4/5, and on accuracy 5/5.
   557→This is consistent with the efficient market hypothesis: closing odds incorporate
   558→vast amounts of information from professional bettors, market makers, and historical patterns.
   559→
   560→The XGBoost models consistently UNDERPERFORM raw market by +0.12-0.20 log loss points.
   561→This means our ML models add noise to an already well-calibrated signal.
   562→The ELO model (LL ~1.01-1.03) is the best football-only model, outperforming
   563→XGBoost football-only (LL ~1.00-1.16) in most windows, suggesting that 46 engineered
   564→features introduce overfitting that outweighs their informational value.

   597→### 5. What Would Improve ELASTICO Most
   598→
   599→If the market already encodes most predictable information, the path to
   600→differentiation is NOT more complex algorithms. It is:
   601→
   602→1. **Better football data** — xG from Opta/StatsBomb, not shots-on-target proxy
   603→2. **Injury/suspension data** — missing from current features
   604→3. **Pre-match news sentiment** — not currently captured
   605→4. **Live/in-play modeling** — different from pre-match
   606→5. **Market timing** — early odds vs closing odds movement as a feature
   607→
   608→The LSTM failure confirms this: temporal football modeling with proxy features
   609→cannot beat simple baselines. Better data, not better algorithms, is the constraint.
