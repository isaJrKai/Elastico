   575→))

   577→story.append(h2('12.4 In-Memory State in Serverless'))
   578→story.append(body(
   579→    'Several systems maintain state in memory: rate limiting (Map in rate-limit.ts), AI provider cooldowns (Map in ai-gateway.ts), and notification counters.
